1. Who we are

APP_NAME ("the app") is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at salehabdelrahman119@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following in a local database and in files inside the app's private storage on your device:

  • Chats and messages, including pictures and files you attach.
  • Characters you create: name, picture, personality, voice settings, memories, and documents you upload as knowledge.
  • Your profile name and picture, if you set them.
  • Settings, including the address and API key of each AI provider you add. API keys are stored only on your device and are never shown in full in the app or written to logs.
  • Downloaded speech models and a cache of spoken audio.

We cannot see or access any of this data.

3. When data leaves your device

The app only connects to services that you set up or start. Each of these services has its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to read it.

ServiceWhat is sentWhen
Your AI model provider (an OpenAI-compatible endpoint you enter, for example LM Studio or Ollama on your own computer, or a hosted service such as OpenRouter or OpenAI) The text of your conversation, the character's description, relevant excerpts of your uploaded documents, pictures or files you attach, and your API key for that provider Each time you send a message or the app writes a reply
Web search (DuckDuckGo or a SearXNG instance you choose) Search queries and the addresses of web pages to read Only when you turn on web search for a chat
Model download hosts (Hugging Face and similar) A normal download request for the model files Only when you start a model download
Your own PC running APP_NAME, on your local network Chats, characters, settings, and, if you use the PC for speech, your voice audio Only after you pair your phone with your PC (for example by scanning its QR code)

We do not operate any of these services and we do not receive copies of what is sent.

4. Microphone

The app uses the microphone for voice input and live voice chat. Speech is turned into text on your device by the Whisper model. Audio is not recorded to storage and is not sent anywhere, unless you choose to let your paired PC do speech recognition, in which case it goes to that PC on your local network.

5. Camera

The app uses the camera only to scan the QR code that connects your phone to your PC. The image is processed on your device, is not saved, and is not sent anywhere.

6. Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking code, and does not use advertising IDs. We do not sell or share personal data with anyone.

7. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Connections to hosted AI providers, web search and model downloads use HTTPS when the service supports it. If you point the app at a server that uses plain http:// (common for AI software running on your own computer), that traffic is not encrypted, so only do this on a network you trust. Pairing with your PC can be protected with a shared key.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete single chats, characters, documents and memories in the app, export or remove all data under Settings → Data and backup, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data you have already sent to a third-party service is handled by that service's own policy.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Because the app can connect to AI models chosen by the user, which may produce unexpected content, it is intended for users aged 13 and older.
